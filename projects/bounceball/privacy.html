Bounce Ball privacy policy

Last updated October 11, 2026

This policy covers the Bounce Ball mobile app for iOS and Android, published by Abidur Chowdhury. You don't need an account to play.

Information collected

Bounce Ball does not collect, store, or share personal information. Nothing you do in the app is sent to me or to anyone else.

On your device

Your high score and total time played are saved only on the device. That data stays there, and it is deleted when you delete the app. It is not backed up to a server I control, because there isn't one.

Ads, analytics, and third parties

Bounce Ball has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. It does not use third-party services.

Permissions

The app does not ask for permissions. It does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, or files.

Children's privacy

Bounce Ball collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. The page address will stay the same.

Contact

Questions about this policy: email@abidur.net
